Welcome to CoachView

CoachView is a starter workout video platform. It includes registration, login, coach-only video uploads, and a video list view.

To begin, register as a coach or viewer. Coaches can upload videos, viewers can browse uploaded videos.

  • Health endpoint: /api/health
  • Register endpoint: /api/auth/register
  • Login endpoint: /api/auth/login
  • Videos endpoints: /api/videos and /api/videos/:id
